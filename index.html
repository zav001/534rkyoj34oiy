What is DonutClient?

DonutClient is a Fabric-based Minecraft client focused on DonutSMP and Minecraft 1.21.11.

Install Fabric for 1.21.11, place DonutClient-1.21.11.jar in your mods folder, and launch the matching Fabric profile.
